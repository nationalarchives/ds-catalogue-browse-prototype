Understanding the catalogue

Records held at The National Archives are organised by the government department which created them.

Navigating the records

Select a level to see the records within it. The actively selected level is highlighted in green.

Catalogue hierarchy

There can be up to seven levels in the hierarchy.

  • Department: The government department of origin.
    • Division: Records of a particular section of a department.
      • Series: Records that were created or used together.
        • Sub-series: Group of related pieces with a common function or subject within a series.
          • Sub sub-series: Group of related pieces with a common function or subject within a sub-series.
            • Piece: Units within a series, such as a file or volume.
              • Item: Sub-divisions of a piece, for example a letter in a file or a photograph in an album.
